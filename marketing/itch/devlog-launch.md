Title: Killi and Milli is out

Killi and Milli is a co-op reef maze for one or two players, and it is playable now in the browser on this page.

Two pufferfish are in charge of the snacks for the reef's Moonlight Feast, and the night before, something wakes up in the Trench. Your only tool is coral: grow a wall, break it to get through, and puff up when a creature gets too close. Sixteen reefs each add one twist, Bruiser the shark guards the way down, and the Kraken Queen waits at the bottom.

A few things I am proud of. Every sound effect and all five music loops are synthesized in the browser as you play; the game ships no audio files. The cut paper look is drawn in code, piece by piece, with each card casting its own shadow. And the whole game is one HTML file with no build step, which is why it loads in a blink.

If you play, tell me in the comments which reef gave you the most trouble. That is what the next update will be about.
